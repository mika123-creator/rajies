About Rajie’s Barber Shop

Located right in the heart of Mitchells Plain at Morgenster Shopping Centre, Rajie’s Barber Shop delivers a top-tier grooming experience without the pretension. We specialize in contemporary hair styling and timeless barbering traditions for men, teens, and children.

Whether you need an ultra-smooth skin fade before the weekend, a disciplined beard shape-up with hot towel treatment, or a clean school cut for your boy, our attention to detail and hygienic tools ensure you leave feeling confident and looking immaculate.
